Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Short version: We only collect what we need to run the app — your account info, the trips and content you create, and how you interact with the app to improve it. We don't sell your data, we don't track you across other apps, and we don't share your information with advertisers.

1. Who we are

Ouest is a social travel planning app operated by Ouest Travel ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our iOS app and website (ouest.travel).

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo, handle, bio, nationality, and travel interests you choose to share.
  • Content you create: trips, itineraries, activities, photos, journal entries, polls, comments, expenses, and other content you add to the app.
  • Social interactions: follows, likes, saves, trip memberships, comments, and direct messages.
  • Device information: device type, iOS version, app version, and an Apple-provided device token used to deliver push notifications.
  • Usage data: how you interact with features (e.g., which screens you visit, what you tap) — used in aggregate to improve the product.
  • Photos: only the images you explicitly choose via the system photo picker. We never access your full library.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate sessions.
  • To deliver core features — trip planning, sharing, comments, notifications.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To improve the app — find bugs, understand which features are useful, and prioritize new ones.
  • To enforce our terms (e.g., prevent abuse, spam, or fraudulent activity).

4. How we share information

We do not sell your data. We share information only:

  • With other users you choose to share with — for example, members of a trip you create can see the trip's content. Public profiles are visible to anyone using Ouest.
  • With service providers we use to run the app — Supabase (database, authentication, storage), Apple Push Notification Service (push delivery), and standard hosting infrastructure. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality.
  • When required by law — to comply with valid legal process (subpoena, court order) or to protect rights and safety.

5. Your privacy controls

  • Privacy mode: you can mark your profile as private from Settings — only approved followers can see your trips.
  • Photo permissions: we use Apple's system photo picker, so you choose individual photos to share — we never read your full library.
  • Notifications: you control which push notifications you receive in iOS Settings.
  • Account deletion: you can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, trips, and content.
  • Data export: contact us to request a copy of your data.

6. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps and websites. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs. We do not use the iOS IDFA.

7. Children

Ouest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn we have, we will delete the account and the data.

8. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your information within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

9. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Authentication tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. Supabase enforces Row Level Security so users only see data they're authorized to access. No system is perfectly secure — please use a strong, unique password.

10. International data

Our servers are operated by Supabase and may process data in the United States or other countries. By using Ouest, you consent to this transfer.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you in the app or by email before the changes take effect.

12. Contact us

Questions, requests, or privacy concerns: hello@ouest.travel — or visit our support page.
