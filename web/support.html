Frequently asked

I shared a trip link but my friend can't open it

If you're sharing a link from before our Universal Links update, your friend may need to open the app and use the "Got an invite code?" field on their home screen, then paste the 8-character code from the share text.

New links sent via iMessage, WhatsApp, or any other messenger should open the app directly when tapped (assuming the app is installed). If they don't have the app yet, the link takes them to the App Store.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → Profile tab → Settings (gear icon) → Account → Delete Account.

This is permanent and removes your profile, trips, photos, comments, and all other content within 30 days.

How do I make my profile private?

Profile → Settings → Privacy → toggle Private profile. Only people you approve as followers will be able to see your trips and travel content.

I uploaded a photo but it's not showing

Photos sometimes take a few seconds to process. Pull down to refresh on the trip or profile screen. If the photo still doesn't appear after a minute:

  • Check that you're connected to the internet
  • Try uploading a smaller image (we cap uploads at ~2MB and auto-compress, but very large originals can fail)
  • Make sure you've granted the app access to your photo library
How do trip invites work?

From a trip's detail screen, tap the Share icon. You can either send a link (which opens the app directly when your friend taps it) or share a QR code for in-person invites.

Each invite has a unique 8-character code. Codes can be set to expire or be limited to a fixed number of uses by the trip owner.

How are trip stickers earned?

Stickers unlock automatically as you use the app — for example, creating your first trip, adding 10+ photos, or visiting 5+ destinations. Tap any earned sticker to see its details and equip it on your profile banner.

You can equip up to 4 stickers at a time.

Can I use Ouest on Android or web?

Right now we're iOS-only while we focus on getting the core experience right. Android is on the roadmap. Sign up for updates at app.ouest.travel.

Found a bug or have feedback?

Email hello@ouest.travel with a description of what happened and a screenshot if you can. We read every message.
